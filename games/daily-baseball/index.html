• You pitch the top of each inning from behind the catcher: choose a pitch, pick your spot, then STOP the needle in the gold — the gold sits somewhere random every pitch. Paint it and he's got no chance; miss and it's hittable or a ball.
• You bat the bottom: SWING when the needle hits the green. Right in the green = crushed. Early or late = weak contact or a whiff.
• Win = more runs than the CPU. Ties count as a loss.
